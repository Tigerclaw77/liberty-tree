## 1. Executive Summary

This sample packet organizes public documentation for Tri-Flow Superior Lubricant Aerosol. The public product page identifies the product, lists 6 oz and 12 oz aerosol SKUs, and states that the product is formulated with P.T.F.E. [`SRC-001`, `EVID-001`, `EVID-002`].

Public SDS/download materials are incomplete for a clean evidence packet. The product page's English SDS link resolves to a document titled "TRI-FLOW Superior Foaming Lubricant with PTFE" with product code `TFF020015`, while the MSDS English link resolves to "TRI-FLOW Industrial Lubricant" for the `TF20000 SERIES` and lists product numbers that appear related but do not exactly match the public SKU strings shown on the product page [`SRC-002`, `SRC-003`, `GAP-002`].

The reviewed public sources do not include a signed PFAS supplier declaration, analytical PFAS test report, full formulation/BOM, or legal review of any regulatory applicability. These missing records are treated as evidence-readiness gaps, not as compliance failures [`GAP-001`, `GAP-003`, `GAP-004`, `GAP-005`].

Evidence-readiness posture: incomplete demonstration record. Public sources are sufficient to show how Liberty Tree would index product information, SDS candidates, PFAS-relevant terminology, and follow-up gaps. Public sources are not sufficient to support a compliance claim.

## 11. Gap Assessment

### Documentation Coverage

The public product page provides product and SKU information, but a complete evidence packet would require supplier-confirmed SDS mapping, a supplier PFAS declaration, formulation-level support, and analytical records if testing is in scope [`GAP-001` to `GAP-004`].

### SDS Currency And Product Match

Two public SDS candidate documents are available through the product page download table, but neither can be treated as a clean product-specific SDS match without supplier confirmation. This is a material evidence-readiness limitation because SDS observations are only useful when the SDS applies to the exact product and revision under review [`GAP-002`].

### Regulatory Citation

The SDS candidate for `TF20000 SERIES` references TSCA 5(a)2 final significant new use rules and 40 CFR 721.10536. The eCFR page is indexed as an authoritative but unofficial current source, but the sample packet does not summarize obligations or apply the rule to the product [`REG-CIT-001`, `GAP-005`].
