# Assumptions And Limitations

## Demonstration Status

This sample packet is a public-source demonstration of Liberty Tree's PFAS Evidence Packet structure. It is not client work, legal advice, regulatory certification, product certification, or a determination that a product complies or does not comply with any PFAS-related requirement.

## Scenario Selected

The sample uses Tri-Flow Superior Lubricant Aerosol because public sources include:

- A product page identifying the product and SKU rows.
- Public product wording that references P.T.F.E.
- Public SDS/MSDS links.
- Public supplier/contact information.
- Public agency and regulatory context that can be source-indexed without drawing a compliance conclusion.

## Assumptions

- Public web sources accessed on July 7, 2026 are treated as the only available source set.
- No supplier outreach was performed.
- No private data room, purchase records, formulations, bills of material, supplier declarations, or laboratory reports were available.
- Product-page SKU strings are recorded as shown in the public source.
- SDS/MSDS records linked from the public product page are treated as candidate documents unless product-to-SKU matching is confirmed by the supplier.
- EPA and eCFR sources are indexed only for general context and citation control.
- The sample packet intentionally preserves uncertainty rather than resolving uncertain facts by assumption.

## Limitations

- The packet does not prove PFAS presence, PFAS absence, or product compliance.
- The packet does not decide whether PTFE, PFOA-related language, or any other fluorinated substance triggers a specific legal obligation.
- The packet does not interpret 40 CFR 721.10536 or any other regulation.
- The packet does not assess state, federal, international, sector-specific, customer-specific, or retailer-specific PFAS requirements.
- The packet does not verify whether the public SDS/MSDS documents are the current correct SDSs for `TF200050` or `TF200060`.
- The packet does not verify supplier identity, corporate ownership, manufacturing location, import status, or distribution markets.
- The packet does not include analytical testing.
- The packet does not include a signed supplier declaration.
- The packet does not include full formulation, concentration, CAS, or source-of-PTFE documentation.

## Quality Controls Applied

- Every substantive sample statement is tied to a source ID, evidence ID, gap ID, action ID, or regulatory placeholder.
- Unavailable information is marked as unknown, pending, or missing.
- SDS silence is not treated as proof of PFAS absence.
- Product/SDS mismatch is treated as an open documentation gap.
- Regulatory citations are indexed without applying them to the product.
- Risk language is limited to evidence-readiness risk.

## Public Source Sufficiency Assessment

The public source set is sufficient to demonstrate the evidence packet workflow. It is not sufficient for a complete PFAS compliance-readiness file.

Minimum additional records needed for a stronger packet:

- Supplier-confirmed current SDS for each product SKU.
- Product-specific PFAS declaration signed by an authorized supplier representative.
- Clarification of PTFE identity, concentration range, formulation status, and whether it is intentionally added under the applicable review definition.
- Distribution and market scope assumptions.
- Qualified legal or regulatory citation review.
- Analytical PFAS test report, if testing is scoped or required by the intended use.
