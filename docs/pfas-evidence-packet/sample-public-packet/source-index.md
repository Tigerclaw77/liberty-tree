## Source Quality Notes

- Public product and SDS sources are enough to demonstrate evidence indexing, but they are not enough to close PFAS readiness gaps.
- Product-page wording and SDS titles/codes do not fully align. The sample treats this as a documentation gap rather than resolving it by assumption.
- EPA and eCFR sources are indexed only for background and citation control. The sample does not summarize or apply regulatory obligations.
